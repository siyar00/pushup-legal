Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

1. Controller

Siyar Yamin

Am Pfenningberg 58a, Plesching, Österreich

Email: yaminsiyar@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No ads, no tracking, no sale of data.
  • Without an account, all workouts stay on your device only.
  • Sensor data (proximity and motion sensors) is processed on the device only and never stored or transmitted.
  • You can export and permanently delete your account and cloud data in the app at any time.

3. Use without an account

Workouts, settings and daily goals are stored locally on the device. Nothing is transmitted to us.

4. Use with an account – which data?

  • Account data: email address, password (stored only hashed), username, optionally display name, profile picture and country.
  • Training data: exercise, repetitions, duration, time, workout type, counting method.
  • Social data: friendships and requests, groups, challenges, blocks and reports.
  • Security data: IP address and time of sign-in attempts (abuse protection), deleted after 24 hours at the latest.

5. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

  • Providing account, sync, friends, groups and challenges: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Appearing in public rankings (country, region, continent, world): only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), off by default and revocable any time in your profile.
  • Abuse and attack protection (rate limits, reports): legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

6. Who sees what?

  • Signed-in users: username, display name, profile picture and country (for search).
  • Friends: your training results (friends ranking).
  • Group and challenge members: your repetitions in the respective period.
  • Public rankings: only with consent.
  • Your email address is never visible to other users.

7. Recipients / processors

  • Supabase Inc. (database, authentication, file storage), servers in Frankfurt am Main (EU), under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).
  • Apple and Google as distribution platforms under their own privacy policies.

We do not transfer data to third countries; where Supabase uses sub-processors outside the EU, this is based on EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Retention

Data is kept until you delete your account. After deletion all data is removed immediately; it may remain in the hosting provider's backups for up to 30 days. Sign-in logs are deleted after 24 hours.

9. Device permissions

  • Proximity and motion sensors: only to count repetitions, processed locally.
  • Camera and photos: only when you actively choose a profile picture.
  • Vibration and sound: workout signals.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and to withdraw consent at any time. Export and deletion are available directly in the app under Profile. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority: Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, www.dsb.gv.at.

11. Minimum age

Use with an account is permitted from age 16; younger persons only with parental consent.

12. Security

Encrypted transmission (TLS), sign-in stored in the device keychain, database-level access control (Row Level Security) and limits on sign-in attempts.

13. Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available in the app.
